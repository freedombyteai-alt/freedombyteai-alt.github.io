CS2 Clip Publisher

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

CS2 Clip Publisher is a desktop tool for Windows that publishes a creator's own Counter-Strike 2 gameplay clips to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works

The clips are rendered locally from the creator's own stream recordings. Every morning the tool shows the next prepared clip with its caption. The creator previews it, chooses the privacy level and the interaction settings, discloses commercial content when the clip contains a paid partnership, and confirms the post. One confirmation publishes exactly one clip; nothing is published automatically or in bulk.

What it does not do

No likes, comments, follows or feed browsing, no access to other users' data, no automated engagement of any kind.

Data

The tokens and settings stay on the creator's computer and are used only to publish the creator's own clips. See the Privacy Policy.
